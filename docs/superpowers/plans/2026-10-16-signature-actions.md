# Signature actions: first resident pass

The approved catalogue bases now gain short, character-specific procedural actions.
Keep the existing pixel density and original geometry. A timer-free, Qt-free
action evaluator drives real named appendages; the resident owns all scheduling.

## Scope and decisions

Use smooth, finite planted-feet actions for all fourteen catalogue-style pets
(including Marmalade). New real joints articulate antennas, folded wings, the
acorn cap, book covers/bookmark, a succulent leaf and selected decorative arms.
An articulated decorative arm does not imply IK reach capability. Preserve all
world-space soles, trusted fixed asset selection, real anatomy capability masks,
pause and interruption, and eventual timer quiescence.

Full supported flops, sitting, hopping, owned star-card/crumb/leaf props and the
four experimental locomotion families remain separate support/ownership work.
The first actions for those pets are explicitly smaller expressions: perk/wag,
nose survey, ear settle, lens survey and arm balance. Do not describe those as
the deferred actions.
