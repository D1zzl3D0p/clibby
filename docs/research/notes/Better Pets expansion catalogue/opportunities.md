# Audience expansion opportunities and original pet concepts

## What does the broader category demonstrate, and what remains a hypothesis?

### Takeaway
Existing products demonstrate several distinct companion promises: caring for something quiet, watching an autonomous worker, expressing an identity, collecting unfamiliar creatures, and interacting with a character. Better Pets can explore those promises through original small procedural characters. These examples do **not** establish that any proposed audience is underserved or would buy Better Pets.

### Cited Findings
- **A quiet nature companion is an established product proposition.** Viridi offers real-time succulent growth, names and care; its publisher explicitly suggests leaving it open while working or returning for a short daily check-in. Its plants can die from incorrect watering, so absence of care pressure is a possible differentiation, not something to inherit automatically. — [Viridi, publisher store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/375950/Viridi/)
- **An autonomous worker can be a companion.** Rusty's Retirement features robots planting, watering and harvesting at the bottom of the screen. Its developer offers zooming for a less distracting view, a Focus Mode that slows production, and vertical placement. This is direct evidence that visibility and tempo are part of the product proposition. — [Rusty's Retirement, developer store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2666510/Rustys_Retirement/)
- **Non-mammal creatures already anchor desktop products.** My Little Spider places a pet spider anywhere on the desktop, collecting bugs while the user works or relaxes, with web decoration and hats. This supports considering invertebrates and a defined habitat; it does not establish demand for realistic spiders or gothic design. — [My Little Spider, developer store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3854330/My_Little_Spider/)
- **Identity, discovery and companionship are sold together.** Spirit City combines collectible pettable spirits with avatar/room customization and focus tools. Its advertised customization includes horns, hoodies, books and room decor, while spirits are discovered through combinations of activities and settings. — [Spirit City: Lofi Sessions, developer store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2113850/Spirit_City_Lofi_Sessions/)
- **Character attachment and desktop affordances are distinct attractions.** Desktop Mate advertises window sitting, cursor play, head pats, alarms and licensed character DLC. Its description also offers sizing and edge tucking. This is evidence of character-specific attachment and placement controls, not a reason to copy licensed mascots or promise matching OS integration. — [Desktop Mate, developer store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3301060/Desktop_Mate/)

### Inferences
- **Opportunity hypothesis:** people unmoved by conventional cute animals may respond to mechanical, botanical, graphic, gothic or object companions. The evidence establishes adjacent product forms, not the size of these groups.
- **Opportunity hypothesis:** users who reject pet apps because they expect attention demands may respond to a companion that is content to be ignored. Slow behavior, no neglect penalties and voluntary interaction could be at least as important as a new silhouette.
- **Opportunity hypothesis:** a person may want an attractive animated desk object rather than a simulated dependent animal. A stone, moth or ink shape can broaden the emotional register without needing a complex game.
- **Positioning suggestion:** organize the catalogue around “quiet company,” “curious company,” “strange company” and “playful company,” with filters for motion and personality. Avoid demographic assumptions such as equating adult users with monochrome or technical users with robots.
- **Practical implication:** new species that share the same bounce/chase/pat sequence add less perceived range than characters with different resting poses, tempos and reactions. This is a design judgment to test, not a market finding.

### Gaps
- No user interviews, conversion experiments, sales estimates or unmet-demand measurements were conducted. Do not describe these concepts as validated growth drivers.
- Current Better Pets inventory and exact rendering capabilities are assessed by the coordinator; this research makes no definitive claim about gaps in the repository.
- Product descriptions establish features and positioning, not independently verified effects on focus, wellbeing or stress.

## Which original concepts would create meaningful catalogue range?

### Takeaway
Prototype the first six to test six different emotional registers before filling out a species checklist. All names, forms and behavior details below are **original design proposals**, not observations about existing products. Audience appeal and procedural implementation ratings are hypotheses pending prototype review.

### Cited Findings
- Nature companionship is a plausible category reference because Viridi positions plants as quiet company; it does not prescribe anthropomorphic plant or stone designs. — [Viridi](https://store.steampowered.com/app/375950/Viridi/)
- Mechanical autonomy is a plausible reference because Rusty's Retirement uses robots doing small visible jobs. — [Rusty's Retirement](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2666510/Rustys_Retirement/)
- Nontraditional animals and small habitats are plausible references because My Little Spider centers a spider and web. — [My Little Spider](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3854330/My_Little_Spider/)
- Original fantasy companionship is a plausible reference because Spirit City collects spirits; its existing characters and visual designs should not be copied. — [Spirit City](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2113850/Spirit_City_Lofi_Sessions/)

### Inferences

The references identify adjacent promises only. The following concepts deliberately diverge in form and behavior. “Simple” means plausible with a small procedural shape hierarchy and bounded transforms; it is not a measured effort estimate, battery claim or confirmed match to the current character ABI.

**Coordinator's local architecture finding, received during research:** the present grounded character loader requires exactly two legs. Existing Mallow, Bramble (werewolf) and Corvid (raven) already provide some soft-cute and dark coverage. Consequently the table's shape complexity must not be mistaken for a cheap current-runtime skin. Treat Rivet's tread, Mossback's four legs, Velvet's perch/flying form, Margin's legless body, Pebblekin's stack, the crab, snail, bat, newt, beetle, cloud and eel as **new locomotion/body-contract investment** unless redesigned. Two-legged Paper Heron, Pocket Observatory, Acorn Custodian, Crumb Golem and Night Librarian are better candidates to explore within the present form. Hearthling could be an explicitly upright two-legged salamander; Rivet could become a two-legged mechanical custodian. Validate each through the local owner. Velvet is an aesthetic contrast within existing dark coverage, not entry into an entirely absent dark niche. This finding comes from the coordinator, not the external sources.

Suggested first-six **audience research** rationale: Mossback tests slow animal affection; Rivet tests mechanical companionship; Velvet tests elegant strangeness; Margin tests graphic minimalism; Hearthling tests approachable fantasy; Pebblekin tests companionship with almost no conventional “pet” cues. This deliberately tests range rather than predicting a popularity ranking. **For a lower-investment production shortlist under the current two-leg constraint**, prefer upright Rivet, upright Hearthling, Paper Heron, Acorn Custodian, Pocket Observatory and Night Librarian. Keep the more radical six as catalogue directions or architecture experiments, not a promise of six inexpensive character additions.

### Gaps
- These are concept briefs, not final art direction or implementation tasks. They need small-size silhouette checks and comparison with current characters before names or proportions are fixed.
- Six prototypes may still be too large a first batch. A practical three-concept sample is Rivet, Velvet and Mossback if the goal is broad visual and behavioral contrast; substitute Margin if “barely animated desk object” is the more important uncertainty.

## Which defaults and scope choices make these concepts valuable rather than distracting?

### Takeaway
Make quiet behavior part of each character's identity. Novelty should be concentrated in a few memorable invitation-based actions rather than constant animation or care chores.

### Cited Findings
- Rusty's Retirement advertises less distracting zoom and slower Focus Mode, making reduced attention an explicit design option. — [Rusty's Retirement](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2666510/Rustys_Retirement/)
- Desktop Mate advertises character size adjustments and edge tucking when unused. These are product precedents, not proof that Better Pets has equivalent desktop integration. — [Desktop Mate](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3301060/Desktop_Mate/)
- Viridi includes maintenance and possible plant death. A companion without deterioration would represent a different product choice. — [Viridi](https://store.steampowered.com/app/375950/Viridi/)

### Inferences
- **Proposed shared default:** silent, modest scale, anchored or slowly roaming, long genuinely still rests, short infrequent idle actions, no cursor pursuit unless invited, no motion around active typing by default. These are recommendations for testing, not established requirements or validated accessibility guarantees.
- **Proposed catalogue controls:** preview each character both resting and interacting; display a plain “mostly still / occasionally wanders / active” motion description. Let personality include confident, aloof, sleepy and observant, not only excitable.
- **Proposed care contract:** a pet remains content while ignored and returns warmly after absence. Do not infer that adding hunger, daily streaks or chores increases value. Those mechanics could turn ambient company into another obligation.
- **Proposed art discipline:** every first-version character gets one readable silhouette, one resting identity, one idle signature and one invited response. Palette swaps are supporting customization, not separate catalogue entries.
- **Proposed engineering boundary:** favor bounded local transforms and a finite inventory of shape poses. Clouds do not need volumetric rendering, moths do not need flocking, an eel does not need fluid simulation and a book does not need a text engine. These are design-level feasibility judgments only; the coordinator must map them to current Qt character support.
- **Proposed validation:** show a still image and a short quiet preview to a few people who reject the existing catalogue. Ask which they would leave visible during normal work and why; ask whether it feels like company, decor or a distraction. Compare across categories rather than asking whether each is “cute.” User evidence is needed before saying any concept entices new users.
- No CPU, RSS, offscreen result or reduced motion proposal here establishes battery savings. Battery impact and desktop parity require appropriate on-device evidence.

### Gaps
- Exact support for arbitrary shapes, layering, long tails, wing folds and stationary characters needs local architecture review.
- No performance or battery measurements were made. Complexity ratings are relative concept judgments, not benchmarks.
- Real OS window perching, global cursor capture, screen interpretation and productivity integrations are separate capabilities and should not be smuggled into a character brief.
