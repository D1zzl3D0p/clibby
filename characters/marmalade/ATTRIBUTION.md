# Marmalade

Original Clibby pixel artwork and four-leg anatomy, authored as editable polygon, line and ellipse recipes in `tools/author_marmalade.py`. No downloaded art, traced sprites, AI-generated images or animation frames are included. MIT licensed.

The atlas contains independently posed attachment pieces. The preview is the authored standing pose; runtime walking, turning, blinking and drop responses are procedural. Seated and flop poses are outside this first anatomy.
